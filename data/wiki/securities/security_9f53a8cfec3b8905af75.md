# Singulus Technologies AG

**Xetra SNG · Yahoo Finance SNG.DE · EUR ordinary shares · ISIN DE000A1681X5**

The first SpaceX heterojunction (HJT) solar-cell line is a material signed order. The valuation depends on the timing of customer acceptance and cash payments, plus repeat orders that sustain utilization after the first delivery.

## Business and contract

Singulus designs and assembles equipment for thin-film deposition and surface treatment. Solar includes crystalline and thin-film production equipment. Semiconductor includes the TIMARIS vacuum deposition platform for specialized applications such as sensors and magnetic memory. Life Science includes medical and decorative coating systems. Large projects make both sales and cash flow sensitive to order and acceptance timing.

On 10 November 2026, Singulus disclosed that its first turnkey SpaceX line has an order value **above €60m** and is due for delivery in H1 2027. The first line alone is equivalent to over 72% of the company's approximately €83m FY2026 sales guidance. The disclosure describes planned additional lines and an ambition to support more than 100 GW. Only the first line is quantified as a signed order. The release gives no milestone billing, profitability, or acceptance schedule, and the model assigns no separate value to the 100 GW ambition.

H1 revenue rose from €31.3m and EBIT improved from −€1.3m year over year. H1 operating cash flow was **−€4.0m** and investing outflow was €1.4m, implying approximately **−€5.4m** before financing. The group had **−€62.6m** of equity at June 30. Completion of projects funded by prior-period advances contributed to the gap between EBIT and cash generation. The latest verified FY2026 company guidance calls for about €83m in revenue and low-single-digit-million positive EBIT. The issuer had scheduled its Q3 report for 11 November. It was unavailable in the financial reports index at the time of review.

June balance-sheet borrowings were approximately €43.5m, comprising €8.9m current borrowing, €31.0m long-term loans and €3.6m in convertible debt. The report describes €29m of five-year secured financing at 5%, with the remaining €2m of long-term borrowing separately disclosed. In October, a further €22.75m subordinated secured SpaceX loan was arranged to repay Triumph loans and project advances. SpaceX is therefore both a major customer and a financier. Applying the announced October loan, assuming €8.9m of already counted Triumph borrowing is retired, and excluding the €3.6m convert upon full conversion gives indicative net financial debt of about €47m. Adding the June pension obligation of €11.3m produces the €58m base-case equity bridge. Actual closing cash, the liability split repaid in October, and current borrowings can move the estimate.

The five-year €3.558m convert carries a 5% coupon and can add up to 1.8m shares. The model assumes full conversion, increasing 8.896527m basic shares to 10.696527m and removing the corresponding debt from the equity bridge. In the bear case, retaining the convertible as debt would also leave modeled equity value at zero.

## Scenario valuation

The reference is the 10 November 2026 Xetra close of **€12.70**, or about €113m of basic equity value. Scenario inputs are underwriting assumptions. They use normalized 2028 operating profit to avoid treating the first delivery as recurring annual sales.

| Scenario | Probability | 2028 sales | EBIT margin | EV/EBIT | Net debt and pension claims | Fair value per share |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Bear | 30% | €90m | 5% | 9× | €65m | €0.00 |
| Base | 55% | €145m | 11% | 12× | €58m | €8.35 |
| Bull | 15% | €230m | 15% | 14× | €55m | €29.60 |

Fair value is the 2028 enterprise value discounted two years at 14% per annum, less current-value debt and pension claims, divided by fully diluted shares. Equity value floors at zero. The base calculation is (€145m × 11% × 12 ÷ 1.14² − €58m) ÷ 10.696527m shares. The **weighted fair value is €9.03**, implying **28.9% downside** from the reference close.

The bear case assumes delays or weak order conversion and renewed liquidity pressure. The base case requires successful first-line acceptance, meaningful follow-on orders or replacement demand, and a margin lift from utilization. The bull case requires several funded lines and much stronger profitability and cash collection. The 30% bear and 15% bull weights reflect the financing and execution burden of a rapid ramp. At the base multiple, claims and discount rate, the reference price requires roughly €21m of normalized EBIT, or about €191m of revenue at an 11% margin. An additional €10m of debt-like claims lowers each positive scenario value by about €0.93 per share. An October mwb research note carried a €10 target before the named SpaceX contract disclosure and serves as a dated cross-check.

## What changes the assessment

First-line delivery, signed acceptance, collection and binding repeat orders matter more to the valuation than announced capacity. A delay, margin shortfall, or payments falling behind project costs weakens the base case. Disclosed profitable follow-ons and sustained positive operating cash flow would support higher assumptions. Confidence is low because the full post-refinancing balance sheet, customer payment milestones and project margins remain unverified. The assessment expires after seven days to reflect the near-term reporting catalyst.
